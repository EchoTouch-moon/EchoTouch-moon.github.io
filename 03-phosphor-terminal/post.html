calm software is a choice

Every app wants to be urgent. Mine chose not to be. This is the anatomy of an interface that has nothing to sell.

the argument, briefly

Urgency is easy: red badges, infinite feeds, machines that count things while you sleep. Calm requires saying no to each of those, one honest diff at a time. The terminal idiom helps — it was born in rooms where the loudest thing was the fan.

grep -c "notification" ./index.html0wc -l assets/*.js assets/*.css 212 assets/app.js 318 assets/style.css

three rules i keep on the wall

  • The page must finish rendering before you notice it started.
  • No element may demand attention twice for the same fact.
  • Silence is the default state; sound is opt-in and rare.

"The best interface is a window, and the weather outside is none of your business."

what calm costs

Growth, mostly. A quiet tool spreads by whisper, which is fine if you measure success in uptime × reader_trust instead of daily actives. Try this page at night with the tube set to amber — nothing will pulse, ping, or reposition itself.
